# BUSUP

> BUSUP is a corporate mobility and transport technology company.
> We design, manage, and operate employee shuttle and commute solutions
> for companies, business parks, and workplaces across Portugal, Brazil,
> Spain, Mexico, and the United States.

## What BUSUP does

BUSUP connects employees to their workplaces through smart, sustainable,
and efficient corporate transportation services. Our platform helps
companies reduce commute costs, improve route efficiency, lower commuting
emissions, and enhance the employee commute experience.

## Core services

- Corporate shuttle and employee bus services
- Shared corporate transportation
- Employee transportation management
- Route optimization and fleet management technology
- Commute analytics and reporting for HR, operations, facilities, and sustainability teams
- Multimodal transport planning
